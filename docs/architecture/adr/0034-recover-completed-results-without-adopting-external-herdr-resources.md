# Recover completed results without adopting external Herdr resources

A completed Managed Agent Pass keeps its correlated output and Result Envelope retrievable by Operation identity until AW acknowledges a successful SQLite ingestion transaction, allowing restart reconciliation to recover completed work without rerunning the agent. Reconciliation may inspect Herdr broadly but may correlate or mutate only resources proven to belong to the exact AW Workflow and Operation; user-created or externally managed panes, sessions, processes, workspaces, and worktrees remain Unmanaged Herdr Resources even when their names, paths, repositories, or branches resemble AW-owned resources. Contradictory or missing ownership produces a notification and ordinary Recovery Plan rather than guessed adoption, broad cleanup, or manual database repair.
