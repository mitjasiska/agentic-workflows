# Recover blockers through ordinary commands

Every blocked, invalidated, interrupted, or otherwise recoverably failed operation exposes a durable Recovery Plan through `awctl status` and the dashboard, while preserving the worktree, sessions, and available evidence. `awctl resume` is the single general continuation entry point for a retained Workflow that stopped before its next safe action. It revalidates current facts, reconciles the preceding immutable Operation, and starts the next Operation only when one safe continuation is unambiguous; otherwise it presents the applicable choices without guessing. A partially completed Submission continues by rerunning `awctl pr`, `awctl continue` remains specific to granting another pass budget, and `awctl cleanup --force` remains the discard path. AW does not prevent human or external-agent intervention, invent a bespoke repair workflow for each error, or require manual database edits. Recovery does not rewrite historical state to erase a blocker. Instead, a successful ordinary action records newer valid evidence that supersedes the blocker for future decisions while preserving the original event and invalidated evidence for audit. This keeps conservative safety checks from recreating the prototype's dead-end states.
