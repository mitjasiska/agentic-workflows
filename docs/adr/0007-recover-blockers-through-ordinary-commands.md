# Recover blockers through ordinary commands

Every blocked, invalidated, or interrupted operation exposes a durable Recovery Plan through `awctl status` and the dashboard, while preserving the worktree, sessions, and available evidence. Recovery reuses a small set of normal workflow commands whose preconditions are revalidated when invoked; AW does not prevent human or external-agent intervention, invent a bespoke repair workflow for each error, or require manual database edits. Recovery does not rewrite historical state to erase a blocker. Instead, a successful ordinary action records newer valid evidence that supersedes the blocker for future decisions while preserving the original event and invalidated evidence for audit. This keeps conservative safety checks from recreating the prototype's dead-end states.
